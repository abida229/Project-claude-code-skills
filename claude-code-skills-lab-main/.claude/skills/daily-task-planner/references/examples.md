# Daily Task Planner Examples

## Example 1: Basic Task List
Input: "I need to finish the quarterly report, attend team meeting at 2pm, and respond to client emails"

Output:
Today is focused with an important report to complete alongside a mid-day meeting.

🗓️ YOUR SUGGESTED DAY PLAN
- 9:00 AM - 10:30 AM: Deep work on quarterly report (high-focus time)
- 10:30 AM - 10:45 AM: Short break
- 10:45 AM - 12:00 PM: Continue quarterly report
- 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM: Lunch break
- 1:00 PM - 2:00 PM: Prepare for team meeting
- 2:00 PM - 3:00 PM: Team meeting
- 3:00 PM - 4:00 PM: Respond to client emails (batch processing)
- 4:00 PM - 5:00 PM: Finalize report details
- 5:00 PM - 6:00 PM: Review and prep for tomorrow

💡 TIPS
- Start the report early when your focus is highest
- Batch email responses to maintain concentration on the report

Review and adjust before finalizing.

## Example 2: User Provides Specific Times
Input: "My day starts at 10am and I have a meeting from 11am to 12pm. I need to write proposal, review contracts, and make phone calls."

Output:
Today has a focused morning with a balanced mix of tasks.

🗓️ YOUR SUGGESTED DAY PLAN
- 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM: Write proposal (high-focus time)
- 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM: Meeting
- 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM: Lunch break
- 1:00 PM - 2:30 PM: Review contracts (medium-focus work)
- 2:30 PM - 2:45 PM: Short break
- 2:45 PM - 4:00 PM: Make phone calls (lower energy activity)
- 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM: Wrap up proposal or handle remaining tasks

💡 TIPS
- Tackle the proposal right after starting to maintain momentum
- Save phone calls for when your energy naturally dips

Review and adjust before finalizing.

## Example 3: Overloaded Schedule
Input: "I have back-to-back meetings from 9am to 4pm and need to finish 3 reports, prepare presentation, and handle admin work."

Output:
Today is heavily packed with meetings and may require task deferral.

🗓️ YOUR SUGGESTED DAY PLAN
- 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM: Back-to-back meetings
- 4:00 PM - 5:00 PM: Begin urgent report (if possible)
- 5:00 PM - 6:00 PM: Handle critical admin tasks

⚠️ WARNINGS
- Heavy meeting load leaves limited time for task completion
- Reports may need to be deferred to tomorrow
- Risk of overtime if all tasks must be completed today

💡 TIPS
- Consider delegating some tasks or extending deadlines
- Focus on completing the most critical items today

Review and adjust before finalizing.
